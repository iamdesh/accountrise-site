FAQ

Straight answers for US buyers.

How much do US remote bookkeeping plans cost?

Bookkeeping starts at $10/hr USD (and $12/hr USD for Odoo ERP accounting). Pricing depends on transaction count, number of accounts, catch-up needs, and reporting frequency, with hourly, project, and monthly retainer options.

How does white-label work for US CPA and EA firms?

We work under NDA following your firm's chart of accounts, naming rules, and review checklists so your team receives clean trial balances and reconciliations ready for tax review or advisory.

Do you file US tax returns or run payroll directly?

We focus on bookkeeping and accounting back-office work—preparing tax-ready statements, 1099 vendor summaries, and payroll journal entries—while licensed tax filing and payroll disbursement remain with your CPA or payroll provider.

How do we share access safely?

You invite Accountrise with Accountant-level permissions inside QuickBooks Online or Odoo using your own admin credentials—no bank logins or fund-transfer authority shared.
