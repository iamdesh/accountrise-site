01What outsourced bookkeeping services does Accountrise provide?

We can help with day-to-day bookkeeping, bank and credit-card reconciliations, cleanup or catch-up work, month-end close, financial reports, e-commerce bookkeeping, payroll-related accounting support, QuickBooks Online, Odoo, and agreed CPA back-office tasks. We’ll confirm the exact scope before starting.

02Do you work with US businesses and Canadian clients?

We primarily work with US businesses and CPA firms, and we also support clients in Canada. We agree on responsibilities, communication, timing, and reporting before work begins.

03Which accounting software do you support?

We work with QuickBooks Online and Odoo Accounting, plus connected cloud-accounting and e-commerce workflows. We’ll discuss your setup and access needs before work begins.

04How much does outsourced bookkeeping cost?

Bookkeeping starts at $10 per hour. The final rate depends on transaction volume, the condition of the books, your software, deadlines, and the work you want us to handle.

05Can you work inside our existing accounting process?

We’ll work within the software and process you already use wherever practical. At the start, we agree on responsibilities, access, approvals, questions, and report formats so both sides know what to expect.

06Can Accountrise provide white-label support to CPA firms?

Yes. We can provide white-label bookkeeping, catch-up work, reconciliations, month-end support, and other agreed accounting tasks. We’ll work to the responsibilities and handoffs your firm sets.

07How do you approach AI and accounting automation?

We start by getting the books organized and setting a consistent process. Where useful, we can identify routine tasks that may be automated; someone still needs to review exceptions and final outputs.
