Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you start.

What bookkeeping services does Accountrise provide?

Accountrise supports day-to-day bookkeeping, bank and credit-card reconciliations, cleanup work, month-end close, financial reporting, QuickBooks Online, Odoo accounting, ecommerce bookkeeping and CPA back-office workflows.

Do you work with US businesses and CPA firms remotely?

Yes. Accountrise is positioned as a remote accounting support partner for US businesses, startups, ecommerce companies and CPA firms.

Which accounting platforms do you support?

Core workflows include QuickBooks Online and Odoo Accounting, with experience across cloud accounting and ecommerce-connected workflows.

How much does bookkeeping support cost?

Bookkeeping support is advertised from $10/hr. Final pricing depends on transaction volume, accounting software, cleanup requirements, reporting and the scope of ongoing work.

Can Accountrise support a CPA firm behind the scenes?

Yes. CPA outsourcing support can include white-label bookkeeping, cleanup bookkeeping, recurring accounting operations and other back-office workflows.
