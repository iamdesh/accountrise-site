Start where you are

Messy books rarely fix themselves.

When bookkeeping falls behind, it can become harder to understand cash flow, answer questions, or prepare for a tax professional. The first step is to identify what is missing, what can be reconciled, and what needs clarification.

What the engagement can cover

Cleanup work with a clear purpose.

Each project is scoped to the condition of the books, the periods involved, and the information available. Depending on your needs, support can include:

Historical transaction review

Review and categorize past transactions using the accounting records and supporting documents available.

Bank and credit-card reconciliations

Reconcile agreed accounts against statements and flag differences that require additional evidence or decisions.

Balance-sheet account review

Review selected balances and investigate items such as uncleared transactions or unexplained balances within scope.

Financial reports

Prepare relevant reports from the records after the agreed bookkeeping and reconciliation work is completed.

Open-items summary

Document missing information, unresolved differences, and questions that need owner, management, or tax-professional input.

Organized handoff

Share the agreed reports and a practical next-step list so you or your CPA can continue with a clearer record.

Common questions

Know what to expect before you start.

Every cleanup depends on the state of the records. The initial conversation helps determine what is needed and whether the engagement is a fit.

Ask a question ↗
What is the difference between catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping?

Catch-up bookkeeping brings missing periods up to date. Cleanup bookkeeping reviews existing entries and account balances to identify and resolve issues where supporting records and access are available. A project can involve one or both.

What information do you need to begin?

We typically start with accounting-system access, bank and credit-card statements for the relevant periods, existing bookkeeping files, and any available prior financial reports. The exact document list depends on the project.

Which accounting systems do you support?

Accountrise supports QuickBooks Online, Odoo, Zoho Books, and other cloud accounting workflows, depending on the project's requirements.

How is a cleanup project priced?

The indicative starting rate is $15 per hour. The final estimate depends on the number of periods, transaction volume, accounting system, reconciliation needs, and condition of the records. We can discuss the scope before work begins.

How long will the cleanup take?

Timing depends on how far behind the books are, the quality and completeness of source records, transaction volume, and the number of accounts that need reconciliation. A timeline can be proposed after an initial review.

Do you work with CPA firms as well as business owners?

Yes. Accountrise can provide catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping support to business owners and CPA firms. For CPA firms, the workflow and handoff can be aligned with the firm's review process and client-service needs.

Will the books be ready for tax filing after cleanup?

The goal is to provide better-organized bookkeeping records and reporting for review by the business owner or tax professional. Tax readiness depends on the records available and the requirements of the tax preparer; tax filing is a separate scope unless agreed otherwise.
