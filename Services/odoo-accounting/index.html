Yes. Support can cover ongoing bookkeeping, reconciliations, cleanup and reporting for an existing Odoo accounting workflow. The exact scope depends on the condition and requirements of the file.

Yes. Setup support can include accounting configuration areas such as the chart of accounts, taxes, journals and core accounting workflows described for the engagement.

How much does Odoo accounting support cost?

Accountrise lists support starting at $12/hour and monthly support at $299/month. Final scope and pricing can vary based on transaction volume, complexity and requirements.

Do you work with CPA and accounting firms?

Yes. Accountrise provides remote Odoo accounting and bookkeeping support for accounting firms, including flexible engagements and client bookkeeping support.
