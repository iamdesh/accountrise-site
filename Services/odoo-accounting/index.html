Before we begin

Odoo accounting questions, answered.

If your situation is a little different, bring it to the first conversation. We'll help define what needs to be reviewed and whether it fits our accounting support scope.

Ask a question ↗
What Odoo accounting work can Accountrise handle?

We can support recurring bookkeeping, bank and balance-sheet reconciliations, month-end close tasks, financial reporting, catch-up bookkeeping and accounting-side setup review. The exact tasks are agreed before work begins.

Can you help if we already use Odoo?

Yes. We can review your current accounting workflow and discuss ongoing bookkeeping, reconciliations, cleanup and reporting based on the condition of your records and the access provided.

Does Odoo setup support include custom development?

The service focuses on accounting-side setup and workflows, such as reviewing the chart of accounts, journals, taxes and accounting processes. Custom development, integrations and technical configuration should be scoped separately with the appropriate technical resource.

How much does Odoo accounting support cost?

Hourly support starts at $12 per hour, and monthly support starts at $299 per month. Final pricing depends on transaction volume, record condition, reporting needs and the agreed scope.

Can you bring overdue Odoo books up to date?

Catch-up work can help organize overdue bookkeeping, investigate reconciliation differences and agree on a plan to bring records up to date. Timing depends on the backlog and quality of source records.

Do you work with US CPA firms and Canadian businesses?

The United States is Accountrise's primary market, and Canadian clients are also supported. Accounting-firm work can be scoped around agreed client bookkeeping tasks, recurring work or overflow capacity.
