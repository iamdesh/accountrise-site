Common questions

Make an informed decision about outsourcing.

Clear answers on scope, white-label collaboration, workflow and starting prices.

Ask a question ↗
What accounting work can Accountrise take on?

Support can include white-label bookkeeping, transaction categorization, bank and credit card reconciliations, QuickBooks bookkeeping, cleanup and catch-up work, monthly reporting and defined overflow accounting tasks. The scope is agreed before work begins.

Can Accountrise work under our firm's brand?

Yes. Accountrise can provide behind-the-scenes bookkeeping support within your firm's agreed workflow and client-service model. Responsibilities, handoffs and communication expectations are defined during onboarding.

Can you support existing client files and one-time projects?

Yes. Work can be scoped for recurring client bookkeeping, cleanup or catch-up projects, reconciliations, reporting support or a period of increased workload.

How are workpapers and review handoffs managed?

The engagement begins by agreeing on responsibilities, file conventions, review requirements, communication channels and how exceptions or open items should be documented. Handoff expectations are set with your firm.

What accounting software do you support?

Core workflows include QuickBooks Online and Odoo Accounting. Other systems or connected workflows can be discussed during scoping to confirm fit.

How much does CPA outsourcing cost?

Hourly support starts at $10 per hour and monthly CPA firm support starts at $499 per month. Dedicated or more complex arrangements are scoped individually based on volume, software, deliverables and deadlines.

Does outsourcing replace our firm's CPA or tax judgment?

No. This service is intended to support bookkeeping and defined accounting workflows. Your firm retains responsibility for client relationships, professional review and services requiring its professional judgment or credentials.

Can you support firms in Canada?

The primary focus is US CPA and accounting firms. Canadian engagements may be considered based on the bookkeeping workflow, systems, scope and applicable requirements.
