CPA outsourcing FAQ

Questions firms ask before outsourcing.

Clear answers about scope, workflow and how a remote accounting partnership can fit into your firm.

What does CPA outsourcing include?

White-label bookkeeping, transaction categorization, reconciliations, QuickBooks support, cleanup and catch-up bookkeeping, financial reporting and flexible remote accounting support.

Can you work under our firm's brand?

Yes. The service is designed for white-label support within your firm's workflow and client-service model.

Can you help with messy client books?

Yes. Cleanup and catch-up bookkeeping are part of the stated service scope, with the work organized around the records and requirements of the engagement.

How do we get started?

Start with a consultation. We'll discuss your firm's workflow, systems, workload and the type of support you need before defining the engagement.
