QuickBooks FAQs

Questions before you get started?

Here are straightforward answers about the scope, cleanup process, pricing, and remote support.

Ask about your books ↗
What is included in Accountrise's QuickBooks bookkeeping service?

Depending on the agreed scope, services can include transaction categorization, bank and credit card reconciliations, cleanup and catch-up bookkeeping, month-end support, financial reporting, and CPA coordination.

Can you clean up an existing QuickBooks Online file?

Yes. We can review the existing file, identify bookkeeping periods or reconciliations that need attention, agree on the cleanup scope, and work through the agreed items.

Do you work with CPA firms?

Yes. Accountrise provides remote bookkeeping support for CPA and accounting firms, including recurring client bookkeeping and scoped overflow work. White-label arrangements can be discussed during scoping.

How much does QuickBooks bookkeeping cost?

Hourly support starts at $10 per hour and monthly support starts at $249 per month. CPA-firm work can be scoped separately. Final pricing depends on transaction volume, account complexity, cleanup needs, reporting, and the agreed work.

Do you support businesses outside the United States?

The United States is Accountrise's primary market, and Canadian clients are also served. The engagement scope is discussed based on the business's accounting workflow and requirements.
