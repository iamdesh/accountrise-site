Bookkeeping questions

Know what you are signing up for.

We can discuss the current state of your books, the systems you use, and which tasks make sense to delegate.

Ask about your books ↗
What is included in outsourced bookkeeping?

Depending on the agreed scope, support can include transaction categorization, bank and credit-card reconciliations, review of uncategorized items, catch-up bookkeeping, month-end close support, and monthly financial reports.

How much does remote bookkeeping cost?

Hourly support starts at $10 per hour, and recurring monthly support starts at $249 per month. Final pricing depends on transaction volume, accounting systems, cleanup needs, and reporting requirements.

Can you catch up on overdue bookkeeping?

Yes. We first discuss the periods involved, the condition of the records, and what information is available. Then we can propose a practical scope for the backlog and a path toward recurring bookkeeping.

Which accounting software do you support?

Accountrise works with QuickBooks Online, Odoo, Zoho Books, and other cloud accounting workflows where the engagement and existing system are a fit.

Do you work with CPA firms?

Yes. We can provide remote bookkeeping capacity for CPA and accounting firms, including recurring client books and agreed project-based work.

Do you work with Canadian businesses?

The United States is our primary market, and we also serve Canadian clients. We agree on the accounting work required based on your business, software, and reporting needs.
