Why Is My QuickBooks Bank Reconciliation Off?

A reconciliation difference does not always mean your entire bookkeeping file is wrong. Most mismatches come from a specific transaction, date, beginning balance, or prior-period change. The goal is to find the cause before changing the numbers.

Answer first

Most reconciliation problems are traceable

When QuickBooks will not reconcile, the most useful question is: “What changed between the bank statement and the QuickBooks register?” Common causes include missing bank activity, duplicate entries, wrong dates, incorrect amounts, stale outstanding checks, bank fees or interest that were never entered, and changes to transactions that were already reconciled.

Avoid forcing the reconciliation with an unexplained adjustment. A clean reconciliation should reflect what actually happened in the bank account.

Common causes

10 reasons your QuickBooks reconciliation is off

Work through these from the simplest checks to the historical ones. The right fix depends on the actual transaction that created the difference.

01

The beginning balance is wrong

If a previous reconciliation was changed or the opening balance was entered incorrectly, the current reconciliation can carry forward a difference.

02

A bank transaction is missing

A check, ACH, deposit, card transaction, fee, or transfer may exist on the bank statement but not in QuickBooks.

03

A transaction was entered twice

Duplicate imports or manual entries can make the QuickBooks cleared amount larger or smaller than the bank statement activity.

04

The amount is wrong

Even a small data-entry error can create an exact reconciliation difference. Compare the actual bank amount to the amount recorded in QuickBooks.

05

The transaction date is wrong

A transaction recorded in the wrong period may exist in QuickBooks but not appear in the reconciliation you are currently completing.

06

Bank fees or interest were never recorded

Monthly service fees, wire fees, or interest can be visible on the bank statement without a matching QuickBooks entry.

07

A transfer was recorded incorrectly

Transfers between business accounts can be duplicated, omitted, or entered as income or expense instead of as a transfer.

08

An old outstanding item is no longer valid

Old checks, deposits, or other uncleared activity can remain on the books long after the underlying transaction should have been resolved.

09

A reconciled transaction was edited

Editing the date, amount, account, or other important details of a previously reconciled transaction can affect later reconciliation work.

10

A prior reconciliation was undone or altered

A historical reconciliation change can move the starting point for later months. When the difference is large or persistent, trace the reconciliation history before posting adjustments.

FAQ

QuickBooks reconciliation questions

Why is my QuickBooks bank reconciliation off?

Common causes include an incorrect beginning balance, missing or duplicate transactions, wrong amounts or dates, unrecorded bank fees or interest, old outstanding items, and changes to transactions that were previously reconciled.

What should I check first when QuickBooks will not reconcile?

Confirm the statement ending date and balance, verify the beginning balance, then compare the bank statement to the QuickBooks transaction list for missing, duplicate, or incorrectly dated items.

Should I undo a previous QuickBooks reconciliation?

Do not undo it automatically. First identify what changed and whether the underlying transaction can be corrected without disturbing accurate historical reconciliations.

How often should a small business reconcile its bank account?

Monthly is a common baseline. Higher-volume businesses or businesses that need tighter cash visibility may choose a more frequent review.
