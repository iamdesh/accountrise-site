Quick answer

A bank deposit, a customer payment and an invoice match are related—but not always the same step.

The correct path depends on how the receipt was recorded and how your Odoo accounting is configured. You may need to match a bank statement line to an existing payment, register a payment against an invoice, reconcile the receivable account, or investigate a payment sitting in an outstanding account.

1

Bank line is unmatched

The imported statement has a deposit, but Odoo has not linked it to an existing payment or appropriate accounting counterpart.

2

Invoice remains open

The receipt may exist, but the payment has not been applied or reconciled against the invoice's receivable line.

3

Outstanding receipt remains

A payment may be posted to an outstanding receipts account pending bank matching, depending on the journal configuration.

4

The amount is different

Partial payment, bank fees, payment processor deductions, foreign exchange or a short payment may explain why an exact match is not available.

Diagnosis matrix

Choose the symptom you see in Odoo

Start with the visible status, then test the likely explanation. Menu labels and detailed behavior can vary by Odoo version and setup.

Symptom ABank

The bank deposit is still unmatched

Check whether the payment was already recorded, whether its amount/date/reference aligns with the bank line, and whether a duplicate import exists. Avoid creating a second payment just because the bank line is unmatched.

First evidence: bank statement line + existing payment list.
Symptom BInvoice

The invoice is still marked unpaid

Find the payment record and confirm it uses the correct customer, receivable account and invoice. The cash can reach the bank while the invoice's receivable line remains unreconciled.

First evidence: invoice status + payment journal entry + receivable lines.
Symptom COutstanding

Outstanding receipts balance is growing

Review how the bank journal uses outstanding receipt accounts, whether imported statement lines are being matched to payments and whether older deposits are still in transit or duplicated.

First evidence: outstanding receipts ledger + unreconciled bank lines.
Symptom DDifference

The payment amount does not equal the invoice

Check for a partial remittance, multiple invoices paid together, processor fees, withholding, deductions, foreign-currency movements or a customer short-pay. Record the difference according to its actual nature.

First evidence: remittance advice + fee detail + customer ledger.
Payment difference tool

Calculate the amount that still needs explaining

Enter the invoice amount and what actually arrived in the bank. Use it to quantify a short payment or extra amount, not to decide automatically how to post it.

Important distinction

A residual is not automatically a write-off. It may reflect several invoices, a bank fee, a credit, a foreign-exchange difference or an amount still due from the customer.

Avoid these traps

Five actions that can make the cleanup worse

01

Create a duplicate payment

Do not register cash a second time if a valid payment is already present.

02

Match by amount only

Confirm customer, currency, date, reference and invoice—not just the number.

03

Write off the residual too early

Investigate fees, remittance details, credit notes and remaining customer balances first.

04

Ignore outstanding receipts

Old balances can hide missing bank matching, duplicate postings or in-transit items.

05

Change accounts to force a match

Keep the accounting trail intact and correct the underlying transaction or mapping.

FAQ

Odoo customer payment questions

Why is a customer payment not reconciled in Odoo?+

The bank statement line may not be matched to an existing payment, the payment may not be applied to the invoice, or the transaction may contain a partial amount, duplicate, wrong customer reference or outstanding-account entry. Trace the bank line, payment and receivable entry together.

Why does the invoice remain open after the money reached the bank?+

A bank deposit alone does not guarantee the invoice's receivable entry has been reconciled. Check whether an Odoo payment exists and whether its receivable line is linked to the intended invoice or invoices.

What is an outstanding receipts account?+

Depending on journal configuration, an outstanding receipts account can act as a temporary account between recording a customer payment and matching it to the bank statement. Review the actual journal setup and ledger activity in your database.

Can one customer payment settle multiple invoices?+

A single remittance can cover multiple invoices. Use the remittance detail to allocate the receipt correctly and confirm the total applied amount agrees to the payment, accounting for any legitimate deductions or differences.

Should I write off a small unmatched balance?+

Only after determining what caused it and confirming the adjustment is appropriate under your accounting policy. The difference could be a fee, an unapplied credit, a short payment, a foreign exchange item or an amount still owed.
