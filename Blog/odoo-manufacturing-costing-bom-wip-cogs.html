01 / Before you diagnose

Separate product cost, production performance, and inventory value.

These measures are related, but they answer different questions. Looking at only one number can send the team toward the wrong fix.

Plan▤

BoM / expected cost

What should a unit consume under the assumptions used for the bill of materials and operations? Check quantities, units of measure, scrap assumptions, component cost and routing.

Control question: are assumptions current?
Actual◷

Production performance

What material was actually consumed, what quantity was produced, and how much time or outside processing was recorded? Include scrap, rework and partial production where applicable.

Control question: what changed on the shop floor?
Value◫

Inventory valuation

What amount is reflected in stock valuation and related accounts? Review product category accounts, valuation method, stock moves, journal entries and the relevant accounting configuration.

Control question: do stock and ledger agree?
Margin↗

COGS and profitability

What cost reaches the income statement when finished goods are delivered or sold, given the configured flow? Review the transaction path and do not assume every setup posts identical entries.

Control question: is margin measured consistently?

03 / Operational workflow

A repeatable six-stage Odoo manufacturing costing review

Document who owns each check, which source document supports it, and when unresolved differences must be escalated.

Control path
Stage 01▦

Confirm product costing setup

Review product category, cost method, inventory valuation approach, UoM, company, and relevant stock or production accounts. Confirm whether the product uses standard price, AVCO, FIFO or another supported configuration relevant to your version.

Output: documented baseline assumptions
Stage 02⌘

Validate the bill of materials

Check component quantities, units of measure, variants, BoM versions, substitutions, scrap assumptions, and whether the production order used the expected BoM. Verify that component costs are current enough for the costing purpose.

Output: approved component model
Stage 03◷

Review routing and work centers

Check the operation sequence, expected duration, work-center cost assumptions, recorded time, capacity and any outside-processing or subcontract costs that belong in the unit economics. Avoid treating estimated labor as actual unless the process supports that interpretation.

Output: realistic operation assumptions
Stage 04▣

Reconcile consumption and output

Compare planned versus consumed components and planned versus completed quantity. Review partial production, scrap, returns, rework, substitutions, unrecorded consumption, and corrections made after the order was completed.

Output: validated production records
Stage 05⇄

Trace valuation and journal entries

Open the relevant stock moves and accounting entries. Check the configured stock valuation method, journal, product category accounts and interim/clearing accounts where used. WIP accounting behavior varies by version and setup, so verify the actual transaction rather than assuming a universal posting pattern.

Output: traceable ledger impact
Stage 06⌁

Explain the variance and close

Group differences by cause—price, quantity, time, yield, valuation or timing. Assign owners and due dates. Reconcile inventory control accounts to valuation reports and verify that COGS follows the configured delivery and accounting flow.

Output: signed-off variance record

05 / Costing controls

Five controls that make the numbers more dependable

01

Use controlled BoM changes

Set a process for reviewing component substitutions, quantities, engineering revisions and effective dates. Retain enough evidence to explain which design or recipe was used for a production order.

02

Review master data after price changes

Decide when purchase-price movement should trigger a standard-cost review or a management cost update. Do not assume a vendor bill automatically revises every product cost field or every previously posted inventory value.

03

Record output, scrap and time promptly

Use clear cutoffs for material consumption, finished quantity, scrap, rework and work-center time. Late records can shift a variance from one period to another and distort unit economics.

04

Reconcile inventory valuation to accounts

At period-end, compare the relevant stock valuation report or detailed valuation records with the GL control account. Investigate timing, manual journals, account mapping, product categories, negative stock and unvalidated or corrected transactions where relevant.

05

Keep a variance owner and explanation

Log amount, root cause, affected orders, proposed action, owner and resolution date. Separate operational fixes from accounting corrections, and approve journal entries through your normal review controls.

07 / Common pitfalls

Mistakes that make manufacturing cost reports harder to trust

Treating the BoM as the actual result

A BoM represents expected structure or consumption rules. Actual consumption, yield, operation time, and cost measurement need their own review.

Assuming one WIP journal pattern

Posting behavior differs across Odoo versions and configurations. Review the actual entries created by your company setup and documented accounting policy.

Changing product cost to force a match

A manual change may hide the originating error or affect future transactions. Trace source documents and apply controlled correction methods.

Ignoring quantity and UoM conversions

A component may be purchased, stocked and consumed in different units. Confirm conversion factors and rounding where values appear unexpectedly large or small.

Closing without reconciling valuation

A clean production order does not by itself prove inventory and GL balances are aligned. Reconcile the reports and investigate reconciling items.

Reviewing only at year-end

Monthly or order-level exception reviews can surface supplier-price drift, high scrap rates and inaccurate operation times before they accumulate.

08 / Frequently asked questions

Odoo manufacturing costing FAQs

Use these as starting points; validate details against your Odoo edition, version, installed apps and accounting setup.

How does Odoo calculate manufacturing cost?+

The costing view can involve component costs from the BoM, operational costs from work centers or recorded operations, and the selected product costing and inventory valuation approach. Exact calculations and accounting entries depend on version, configuration, costing method and how production is recorded. Review actual stock moves and journal entries to confirm how your database behaves.

What is the difference between standard cost, AVCO and FIFO in Odoo?+

They are different cost measurement methods and can affect how cost changes are reflected. Standard cost generally uses a maintained cost; average cost uses a moving average approach; FIFO assigns costs based on the relevant incoming layers. Availability, behavior and consequences depend on product configuration, localization and Odoo version. Choose a method that fits the business and accounting policy, then test representative transactions before going live.

Does Odoo automatically post every manufacturing cost to WIP?+

Do not assume one universal WIP posting pattern. The entries produced depend on the Odoo version, accounting and inventory valuation settings, product categories, manufacturing configuration and process used. Inspect the transactions generated in your database and align them with the company's documented accounting treatment.

Why does the BoM cost differ from the finished product's inventory value?+

The BoM may express expected components or operation assumptions, while inventory valuation reflects the method and transactions actually recorded. Differences may arise from price changes, quantities, scrap, work-center time, cost method, landed costs, timing, valuation settings or the product's accounting configuration. Trace a representative production order from component consumption to finished-product receipt and its accounting impact.

How often should we review production cost variances?+

Set frequency based on production volume, margin sensitivity, cost volatility and close requirements. Many teams review high-value or unusual exceptions during the month and complete a documented variance and inventory valuation review at month-end. Define thresholds and owners rather than treating every variance the same way.

Can an outsourced Odoo accountant help with costing and close controls?+

Yes. Remote accounting support can help review account mappings, reconcile inventory valuation, investigate production-related entries, prepare variance schedules and strengthen month-end documentation. The scope should distinguish accounting review from operational master-data changes and system implementation work.
