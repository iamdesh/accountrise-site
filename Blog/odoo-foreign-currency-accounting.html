First question
Which rate changed?
Quick answer

FX gain/loss usually looks “wrong” because two values are being compared at different rates.

For a foreign-currency receivable or payable, the functional-currency value can change between the original recognition date and later settlement or reporting dates.

01

Original recognition

The invoice is recorded using the applicable exchange rate for the transaction date under the configured accounting process.

02

Reporting or revaluation

An outstanding foreign-currency balance may have a different reporting value when the rate changes.

03

Settlement

When the invoice is paid, the actual settlement amount can differ from the amount originally recognized in functional currency.

04

Accounting result

The difference becomes an FX gain or loss based on the transaction and the configured accounting treatment.

The distinction that clears up confusion

Realized vs. unrealized FX

Businesses often mix these concepts when troubleshooting a month-end report. The cleanest approach is to ask whether the foreign-currency monetary balance has actually been settled.

Common mistakes

Five things to check before calling the FX result “wrong”

01

Wrong date

Comparing rates from different transaction dates.

02

Wrong direction

Reading the quoted rate in the opposite currency direction.

03

Skipped revaluation

Not accounting for an outstanding monetary balance at reporting date.

04

Wrong account

The amount is right but the gain/loss lands in an unexpected account.

05

Manual override

A manual journal or adjustment changes the FX trail.

FAQ

Foreign-currency questions

Why does Odoo show a foreign exchange gain or loss? +

A foreign-currency monetary balance can have a different functional-currency value when the exchange rate changes between recognition, reporting and settlement. The resulting difference can be recognized as an exchange gain or loss under the configured accounting process.

What should I check when Odoo FX gain/loss looks too high? +

Check the foreign-currency amount, original rate, settlement rate, reporting-date rate, transaction dates, prior revaluation or adjustment entries and the accounts used for the resulting difference.

What is the difference between realized and unrealized FX? +

Realized FX generally relates to a foreign-currency balance that has been settled. Unrealized FX generally refers to changes in the functional-currency value of an outstanding foreign-currency monetary balance at a reporting date.

Can exchange-rate changes affect my Balance Sheet even when no payment was made? +

Yes. An outstanding foreign-currency monetary balance can change in functional-currency terms as exchange rates move, so reporting-date accounting may change the Balance Sheet and the related exchange gain/loss.
