The fast answer

Your Balance Sheet can be wrong even when the asset invoice is correct.

The number on the Balance Sheet is the end result of several events. To find the difference, reconcile the asset's full life cycle instead of checking only the latest depreciation entry.

01

Was the purchase capitalized correctly?

Review whether the transaction was classified and capitalized according to the business's accounting policy.

02

Did depreciation start at the expected time?

Compare the intended in-service or capitalization timing with the schedule actually being used.

03

Does accumulated depreciation tie?

The accumulated depreciation balance should be explainable from the asset schedule, posted depreciation and any adjustments.

04

Were disposals handled completely?

A sold, scrapped or retired asset can leave stale cost or accumulated depreciation when the full disposal trail is incomplete.

Troubleshooting map

Six patterns that make an Odoo asset balance look wrong

Use the visible symptom to decide where to start—not where to finish.
01 Cost

Fixed asset cost is too high

Look for duplicate capitalization, duplicate vendor bills, manual journals or costs assigned to the wrong asset.

02 Schedule

Depreciation started too early

Compare the business's intended timing with the depreciation start date actually used in the asset record.

03 Estimate

Monthly expense is not what you expected

Check useful life, residual value and the depreciation method before changing journal entries manually.

04 Accumulated

Accumulated depreciation does not tie

Reconcile posted depreciation, reversals, prior adjustments and the number of periods the asset has been active.

05 Disposal

Disposed assets still appear on the Balance Sheet

Trace the disposal date, asset cost, accumulated depreciation and any resulting gain or loss entry.

06 Close

Subledger and GL disagree

Compare the fixed-asset register to the general ledger rather than adjusting either side blindly.

Common US business scenarios

Office / professional services

Large equipment purchases

Computers, furniture, equipment and leasehold improvements can create a recurring month-end reconciliation task when additions are posted inconsistently.

Control: additions + capitalization date + current-period depreciation.
Construction / field services

Vehicles and heavy equipment

High-value equipment can create meaningful differences when acquisitions, disposals or repairs are classified differently across periods.

Control: fixed asset register + disposal trail + policy review.
Growing business

Many additions every month

The bigger the asset register becomes, the more important a repeatable reconciliation process is for catching duplicates, missing assets and stale balances.

Control: standardized asset intake and monthly review ownership.

FAQ

Fixed asset questions

Why does my Odoo fixed asset balance look wrong? +

The difference may come from asset additions, capitalization timing, depreciation assumptions, accumulated depreciation, disposals or manual adjustments. Reconcile the asset's full life cycle to the general ledger.

How do I reconcile accumulated depreciation? +

Start with the opening accumulated depreciation, add current-period posted depreciation, then review disposals, reversals and other adjustments. The resulting amount should agree with the relevant Balance Sheet account.

Why is depreciation expense higher or lower than expected? +

Check asset cost, useful life, residual value, depreciation method, start date, prior adjustments and which assets were active during the period.

What should I do with a disposed asset that is still on the Balance Sheet? +

Review the disposal transaction, disposal date, asset cost, accumulated depreciation and any resulting gain or loss. Avoid deleting or force-clearing the balance before understanding the underlying entries.

Is book depreciation the same as tax depreciation? +

Not necessarily. Financial reporting and tax rules can use different methods, lives, conventions and rules. Keep the accounting records aligned with the applicable financial reporting policy and handle tax depreciation separately as required.
