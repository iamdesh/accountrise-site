The core concept

Cash collected, invoice issued, revenue earned: three different events.

When a customer pays ahead of the service or performance period, the unearned portion generally remains a liability until the related obligation is satisfied. The schedule should explain how that balance turns into revenue over time.

Event 01Invoice

The customer is billed

The invoice records the amount due from the customer. For advance billing, that alone does not prove the full amount has been earned.

Event 02Defer

The unearned amount sits in a liability

The appropriate portion is tracked as deferred revenue or another suitable liability based on the contract and accounting policy.

Event 03Recognize

Revenue is recognized as the obligation is satisfied

Periodic entries move the appropriate amount from the liability to revenue based on the service pattern or other applicable recognition approach.

Contract-to-ledger journey

Where the deferral process can break

Treat each stage as a control point. A correct invoice does not guarantee that subsequent deferral and recognition entries are correct.

01 • Contract

Confirm what was promised

Check service dates, billing period, deliverables, renewal terms and whether performance occurs over time or at a point in time.

02 • Invoice

Check account mapping

Verify that invoice lines use the intended revenue or deferred-revenue account treatment for the configured process.

03 • Schedule

Inspect timing and amounts

Confirm the schedule begins and ends in the right periods, total scheduled amounts agree to the relevant balance, and periods are allocated correctly.

04 • Close

Reconcile the liability

Tie deferred revenue to unrecognized contract amounts and explain the current-period revenue released from the liability.

Real-world patterns

When should you investigate deferred revenue?

Use these scenarios to identify where the schedule and service agreement may not agree.

Scenario A

Annual SaaS or support plan

A customer pays for 12 months upfront. If the service is provided over the year, recognizing the full amount in the first month may overstate that month's revenue.

Review: contract period vs. recognition schedule.
Scenario B

Prepaid maintenance

A business bills a maintenance package before all support periods have occurred. The outstanding obligation should be visible and reconciled, subject to the contract terms.

Review: service dates, account mapping and monthly release.
Scenario C

Retainer or advance billing

A customer's payment or advance invoice can be mistaken for earned revenue even when work remains. The correct treatment depends on what has been delivered and the contract terms.

Review: performance completed vs. services still owed.
Common failure patterns

Six causes of a deferred-revenue mismatch

Check these before making a manual adjustment to the liability or income statement.

01

Invoice posted directly to revenue

The setup may recognize the full billed amount before the related service period has elapsed.

02

Schedule has wrong dates

A start/end date mismatch may push revenue into periods outside the contract.

03

Schedule total does not tie

Discounts, credits, partial periods or manual edits may leave part of the amount unallocated.

04

Monthly release did not post

The schedule may exist, but the related period entries may be missing or unposted.

05

Credit note not reflected

A contract change can affect unearned revenue; confirm the schedule reflects the approved change.

06

Liability account does not reconcile

The ledger may include manual entries or contracts not included in the supporting schedule.

FAQ

Deferred revenue questions

What is deferred revenue in Odoo?+

Deferred revenue generally represents an amount billed or received before the related goods or services have been provided. The unearned portion is typically tracked as a liability and recognized as the relevant obligation is satisfied.

Why is Odoo recognizing the full invoice as revenue?+

Check the accounts mapped to invoice lines, deferral setup and whether the appropriate schedule or recurring entries are configured and posted. Exact labels and steps can differ by Odoo version and configuration.

How do I reconcile deferred revenue at month-end?+

Compare the contract-level unrecognized balance with the deferred revenue liability in the general ledger. Tie current-period releases to recognized revenue and investigate credits, schedule differences and manual entries.

Is every customer deposit deferred revenue?+

Not automatically. Treatment depends on the contract, what the payment relates to, whether a performance obligation has been satisfied and the applicable accounting policy. Review the facts before deciding how an advance should be classified.

Can I use a straight-line schedule for every contract?+

No. Straight-line recognition is appropriate only when it reasonably reflects the pattern in which the related goods or services are transferred. Some contracts require another pattern or separate treatment for distinct obligations.
