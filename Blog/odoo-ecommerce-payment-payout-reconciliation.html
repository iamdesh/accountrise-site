01 / The settlement puzzle

A bank deposit is not the same thing as sales revenue.

Payment platforms often send a net amount after deductions. If that net deposit is recorded as revenue without accounting for the underlying activity, sales, fees, refunds and customer balances can be misstated or harder to audit.

Use a documented flow that matches your store and processor setup. Odoo connector availability, fields and automatic posting behavior can vary by version, apps and configuration.

01 / Source▤

Storefront orders

Capture order totals, discounts, shipping charged, tax, cancellations and sales dates. Confirm how order status and refunds are transferred or imported into Odoo.

Control question: is the order population complete?
02 / Deductions−

Processor activity

Identify processing fees, marketplace commissions, refunds, chargebacks, reserve holds and other settlement adjustments using the processor's detailed report.

Control question: can every deduction be explained?
03 / Accounting⌘

Odoo records

Review invoices or sales entries, payment records, credit notes, fee expenses, clearing accounts, and any tax or inventory treatment relevant to the transaction.

Control question: do records represent gross activity?
04 / Evidence✓

Bank settlement

Match the net transfer to the relevant payout ID and bank line. Investigate timing, multiple deposits, currency conversion, reserves and unsettled amounts.

02 / Reconciliation blueprint

Build a six-step payout workflow your team can repeat.

The sequence below is a control framework, not a claim that every store integration automatically performs each step. Adjust it to your commerce platform, Odoo version, payment provider and accounting policy.

STEP 0101

Set the source of truth

Document where order, payment, refund, fee and payout data originate. Define the key ID for matching: order, transaction, batch, payout or settlement reference.

Output: source map + reconciliation key

STEP 0202

Record gross activity

Confirm how orders, discounts, shipping, tax, cancellations and refunds enter Odoo. Avoid using only the net cash deposit as the sales figure.

Output: complete sales population

STEP 0303

Map fees and deductions

Separate processing fees, marketplace commissions, refunds, chargebacks and reserve movements. Agree account mappings and retain support for material or unusual charges.

Output: explainable deductions

STEP 0404

Use a clearing approach

Where appropriate, route processor collections through a dedicated clearing account so the transfer to the bank clears the payout amount rather than netting all activity into sales.

Output: traceable settlement balance

STEP 0505

Match payout to bank

Match by payout ID and expected amount, then investigate differences such as reserves, timing, split transfers, currency exchange or unmatched transactions.

Output: cleared bank deposit

STEP 0606

Review the remaining balance

Review outstanding clearing items, refunds in transit, unsettled sales, disputed payments and reserve balances. Assign an owner and target date to every material exception.

Output: documented exceptions

04 / Exception triage

Select the symptom. Follow the evidence.

Use this quick guide to decide where to look first. Avoid posting a plug entry before the processor report, Odoo records and bank line have been compared.

↗
Suggested investigation

Bank payout is lower than expected

Compare the payout statement with your calculation. Check for fees, refunds, disputes, reserve holds, split payouts, currency conversion, or a transaction that fell outside the date range.

Inspect first

Processor payout detail, payout ID, bank line, and the settlement date range.

Avoid

Booking the unexplained difference directly to sales or miscellaneous expense.

05 / Posting logic

Think in transaction layers—not one net deposit.

The exact journal entries depend on your Odoo configuration, integration and accounting policy, but the underlying questions are consistent. Each type of activity should have a clear home and an audit trail.

Transaction layer
What the accounting review should confirm
Orders and sales
Reconcile order count and total values, including discounts, cancellations, shipping charges and applicable tax. Confirm which records create invoices or sales journal entries in your workflow.
Customer payments
Confirm that receipts are recorded and linked to the right invoices or customer balances where relevant. Avoid creating a second payment entry if an integration already imported the transaction.
Processor / platform fees
Record supported deductions in the appropriate expense or clearing treatment under the business's accounting policy. Reconcile fee reports to the statement instead of estimating fees from a percentage.
Refunds and disputes
Link approved refunds to the original sale where possible, review credit-note treatment and trace the cash movement separately. Chargebacks may require tracking while a dispute is unresolved.
Payout and bank
Match the processor's net settlement to the bank line. Clear the relevant payment clearing account and document any residual amount, timing difference, reserve or currency conversion.
Inventory and tax
For businesses holding inventory, reconcile stock movement and cost of goods sold with the configured valuation method. Review sales tax mapping and filing obligations separately; a payout report alone is not a tax return.

This article provides operational accounting guidance, not legal or tax advice. Confirm state and local sales tax requirements with an appropriately qualified US tax professional.

07 / Common questions

Odoo ecommerce reconciliation FAQs

Practical answers to common accounting questions from growing online sellers.

Why is my Shopify or marketplace sales total different from the bank deposit?+

Bank deposits may be net of processor fees, marketplace commissions, refunds, chargebacks, reserves and adjustments. There may also be timing, currency or date-range differences. Compare the order and transaction reports to the settlement report, then match the payout ID to the bank statement.

Should I record only the net Stripe or PayPal payout as revenue in Odoo?+

Recording only the net payout as sales can hide fees, refunds and other activity. A documented workflow should capture gross sales and separately account for deductions and settlement. The exact entries depend on the integration, Odoo configuration and the company's accounting policy.

Can Odoo automatically import Shopify, Stripe, PayPal or marketplace transactions?+

Available connectors and supported data vary by Odoo version, the installed integration, provider changes and configuration. Before relying on automation, test which order, payment, fee, refund and payout fields transfer, how duplicates are prevented, and whether the imported records support the reconciliation controls you need.

What is a payment processor clearing account used for?+

A clearing account can help separate customer payment activity from the later bank transfer. The processor collection, deductions and net payout can be traced through the account, and the remaining balance should be explainable by unsettled transactions, reserves, timing or other supported items. Design the flow around the actual integration and accounting policy.

How often should an ecommerce business reconcile payouts?+

Choose a cadence based on transaction volume, payout frequency, dispute risk and close requirements. High-volume sellers often benefit from reconciling each payout or on a frequent schedule, then reviewing clearing balances, fees, refunds, sales tax and inventory-related balances at month-end.

Can outsourced Odoo accounting support help with ecommerce reconciliation?+

A remote accounting team can help reconcile sales and payout reports, review fee and refund treatment, investigate clearing-account balances, prepare close schedules and document exceptions. Scope should clarify who owns integration changes, operational decisions, tax advice and final review approvals.
